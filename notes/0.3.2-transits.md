## 2. Search method

The search samples every 20 days. If a station falls in a step and a boundary was within reach, the station is found to the second. Each one-direction piece crosses at most one boundary, and that crossing is narrowed to the second. The reported time is the first whole UTC second in the new sign.

**Why the step is safe:**
- Saturn's stations are never closer than 133.5 days and Jupiter's never closer than 117.5 days (measured 1800–2399).
- In a 20-day step Saturn and Jupiter move at most ~6°.
- The mean node never stations.
- A dense 1-hour scan finds exactly the same crossings:
  - Saturn 1960–2040
  - Jupiter 1990–2040
  - Rahu 2000–2030 (19 crossings)

An earlier version used 5-day steps and plain bisection. It gave byte-identical output for all 29 births at three moments, but was 3× slower. It took 370–480 ms per chart on Vercel, so it was replaced.
